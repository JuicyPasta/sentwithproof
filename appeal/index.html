Questions

Do you write the appeal?

No. This is for people who already have their letter, whether you wrote it yourself, with your doctor's office, or with an AI assistant. We handle delivery and proof.

Why fax?

Most insurers list a fax number for appeals, it arrives the same day, and the confirmation shows the date, time and page count. That record matters if the insurer later says nothing arrived or that you missed the deadline.

Is this legal or medical advice?

No. We are a delivery service. We don't review the merits of your appeal or represent you with your insurer.

What happens to my documents?

They are used to send your appeal and produce your confirmation, and for nothing else. You'll be able to delete them at any time.

Sent With Proof is a document delivery service. It is not a law firm, an insurer, or a medical provider.
